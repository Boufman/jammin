Point the camera so the part number and batch number both sit inside the orange box, then tap the shutter. Detected number-like strings appear as tappable chips — tap one, then tap Part or Batch to assign it. You can also just type directly into either field. If a batch number is present, the barcode encodes PART/BATCH; otherwise just PART.
